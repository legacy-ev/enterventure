Review standards

Built around honesty, relevance, and policy fit.

What we check before submission

  • Advertiser identity, destination URL, contact route, and offer ownership are clear.
  • Creative, image, and landing page claims match the actual product or service.
  • Campaigns avoid prohibited or restricted categories unless the advertiser is explicitly approved.
  • Pages are valid, reachable, mobile-friendly, and do not block relevant review crawlers.
  • Ads are distinguishable from ChatGPT and do not imitate OpenAI product interfaces.

Client categories

Enterventure is best suited to consumer goods, local services, travel and experiences, digital products, education, and other categories that can be represented clearly and responsibly.

We decline or pause work where the offer, claims, destination, or client category would create review risk, user-safety risk, or regulatory ambiguity.
